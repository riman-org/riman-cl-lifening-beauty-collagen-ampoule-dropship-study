### Overview

Background: The introduction of oral nutraceuticals into the Latin American market requires rigorous validation of bioavailability claims against local regulatory frameworks and consumer sensory preferences. This study evaluates the LIFENING Beauty Collagen Ampoule Drink (containing MFDS-certified Collagen Peptide SH 2,000mg) within the Chilean context, focusing on a zero-inventory drop-shipping model for independent planners. Methods: A stratified survey of N=200 participants across Santiago, Valparaiso, and Concepcion assessed Likert-scale perceptions of formulation efficacy, packaging stability, and regulatory confidence, alongside Net Promoter Score (NPS) metrics. Results: Overall composite mean was 3.29 with a Top-2-Box percentage of 41.4%. Key findings indicate strong trust in MFDS certification (Mean 3.83, T2B 76.0%) and ingredient alignment with dermatological trends (Mean 4.04, T2B 85.0%). However, logistical latency concerns dominate barriers to adoption (71.0%), and the liquid ampoule format received low suitability scores for dry climates (Mean 2.2, T2B 17.5%). The NPS score was -19.5, driven by high passive sentiment (73.5%) and detractor rates (23.0%). Strategic Implications: While product quality is perceived as competitive against K-Beauty importers (73.5% benchmark), success depends on mitigating cold-chain integrity risks and clarifying SEREMI labeling compliance. Automated logistics support is critical for planner retention.
